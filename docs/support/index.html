Ballistic Escalation: support

Questions, problems or ideas: email mishaannatech@gmail.com. Please say which phone or tablet you use.

Common questions

  • I bought something on a new phone and it is missing. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. It brings back Premium, Remove Ads and maps, but not coins.
  • Where are my coins? Coins are saved with your game progress on your device.
  • How do I change my ad privacy choices? Settings, Privacy choices (shown where the law requires it).
  • How do I delete my purchase records? Email us and we will ask our purchase service to delete them.
